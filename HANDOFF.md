## Structure
src/pages/Home.jsx is the reference. Every page matches it: wrapper, `.sec` headers with `.sec-n`, `.index` rows, standard close.
`.index-name` = names only. `.index-meta` = sentences.
Classes: wrap pad sec sec-n display h2 h3 lede label gold amber text-mute text-dim btn btn-primary btn-ghost btn-block link index index-row index-n index-name index-meta card border-line field
